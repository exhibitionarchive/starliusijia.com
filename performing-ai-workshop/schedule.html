Workshop Schedule

Two sessions of critique, making, and reflection.

The workshop is designed as two consecutive in-person sessions. Exact timing will follow the final CHI 2027 workshop schedule.

Session 1

Recognizing and Mapping Perceived AI Aesthetics

10 min

Welcome and Introduction

Workshop motivation, framing, and activity overview.
30 min

Participant Introductions and Blind Voting

Each participant introduces their creative practice while their submitted set is shown. Other participants vote on which work appears most AI-generated, with optional ranking from most to least AI-like.
30 min

Reveal, Artist Reflection, and Critique

The production process is revealed. Creators explain their intended AI-like characteristics and participants discuss the visual cues behind their judgments.
20 min

Collective Mapping

Recurring characteristics are organized into an initial shared vocabulary of perceived AI aesthetics.

Session 2

Performing and Challenging AI Aesthetics

10 min

Group Formation

Participants form small groups around themes identified in Session 1, with a mix of creative backgrounds where possible.
35 min

Rapid Visual Prototyping

Each group translates selected AI-like characteristics into a lightweight visual prototype rather than a finished artwork.
25 min

Second Critique

Groups present their prototypes without initially revealing the intended characteristics. Other participants respond to whether and how the work reads as AI-like.
15 min

Reflection and Closing

Participants reflect on the process, possible completion of prototypes, and opportunities for future collaboration and exhibition.
