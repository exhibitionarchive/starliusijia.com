Call for Participation

Bring your own idea of what “looks AI.”

We welcome creative practitioners from a range of visual fields, including artists and designers, as well as HCI researchers interested in AI, creativity, and visual culture.

How to Participate

Instead of a conventional position paper, applicants intending to attend the workshop should submit:

01

Portfolio

A short portfolio or link to selected previous creative work.

02

Human-made work

One original purely human-made visual work, including but not limited to an image, illustration, photograph, digital composition, animation, or short video, intentionally designed to appear AI-generated.

03

AI comparison set

A set of AI-generated works in a similar visual style and format to accompany the human-made work.

04

Short statement

A brief statement describing your creative practice, medium, and production process.

The human-made submission should not use generative AI in the creative process. Accepted participants will take part in blind critique, collective discussion, and rapid critical making activities during the workshop.
