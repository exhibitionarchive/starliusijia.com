Sijia Liu

Sijia Liu is a PhD candidate at the School of Creative Media, City University of Hong Kong. Her research examines generative AI and creative practices from HCI and curatorial perspectives, including human–AI co-creation, artistic workflows, and creative practice around emerging technologies. She has experience organizing artist residencies, exhibitions, and participatory creative research.
